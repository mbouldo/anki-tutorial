## Finding AnKing Cards by Copied Tag

Many third-party resources already map directly to AnKing tag strings. If you have a copied tag, you can bypass having to click through the whole tag tree.

Open the Browser, paste the tag string into the search bar, and press Enter. Anki will filter directly to the matching cards.

Review the results, select all cards, then Toggle Suspend to activate them for study.

## Examples:
All Boards & Beyond Cards (21,179 cards):

 `tag:#AK\_Step2\_v12::#B&B` 

Boards & Beyond GI Cards (1840 cards): 

`tag:#AK\_Step1\_v12::#B&B::09\_GI` 

Boards & Beyond GI Physiology (308 cards): 

`tag:#AK\_Step1\_v12::#B&B::09\_GI::02\_GI\_Physiology` 

Boards & Beyond Bile Video Cards (30 cards): 

`tag:#AK\_Step1\_v12::#B&B::09\_GI::02\_GI\_Physiology::01\_Bile`
