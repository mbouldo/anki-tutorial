## Getting Cards from Amboss Questions

Amboss integrates directly with AnKing by providing suggested Anki cards for specific questions. This lets you quickly convert missed/guessed/confusing questions  into targeted flashcards. Often times if I "had just known that one fact, I would've gotten the question right", which makes getting a flashcard for that question incredibly powerful.

I used this feature a lot in dedicated step stuying (when using Amboss), or during clerkship.

After completing a question, click **“Get Anki Cards”** to generate a set of relevant cards. You can review the suggested list and remove any that are not needed before copying the **Anki query**.

Open Anki, go to the **Browser**, and paste the query into the search bar. This will filter the deck to the matching cards for that question.

From there, review the results, select the cards you want, and **Toggle Suspend** to unarchive them into your main Anking deck.
