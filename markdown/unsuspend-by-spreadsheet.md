## Finding AnKing Cards by Spreadsheet

Some NYUGSOM students maintain a spreadsheet to map lectures directly to AnKing tags. This allows you to create a structured workflow to first look at the spreadsheet to see which third party tags exist for the lecture.

Find your lecture row in the spreadsheet and copy the **“Specific AnKing Tags”** string associated with that topic.

Open the **Browser** in Anki, paste the string into the search bar, and press **Enter**. This filters the deck directly to the relevant cards.

From there, review the returned cards, select the ones you want, and **Toggle Suspend** to activate them for study.

## Note:

You do **NOT** have to do all the cards for each lecture. Usually, these cards include ALL related cards, however, you can just do specific cards within that search query, search by specific third party resources that you already used, or further filter by *high yield* cards.
