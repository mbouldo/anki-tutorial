## Suspending Step 1 Only Cards

Some AnKing cards are tagged for both Step 1 and Step 2 content. During clerkships, it can be useful to isolate and remove Step 1-only material to decrease card burden that is unlikey/never going to be on any future exam. I did this the day after I took step 1 after preclinical year, and it suspended half of my deck (~10k cards).

You end up retaining many cards because there is fundamental overlap between Step 1 and Step 2. Keeping these cards in rotation lets you stay sharp on fundamentals that serve to be foundational in most of your future step 2 / clerkship content - I highly recommend this!

Open the **Browser**, locate the relevant Step 1-only tag, and paste it into the search bar. This filters the deck to cards that are explicitly Step 1 and not cross-tagged with Step 2.

Select all returned cards and **Toggle Suspend** to activate or deactivate them as needed for your workflow. This helps reduce overlap and keeps your study set aligned with your current exam focus.
