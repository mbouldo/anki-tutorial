## Finding AnKing Cards by Shelf Exam

AnKing also organizes cards by shelf exams using structured tags under the Step 2 tag tree. This allows targeted studying based on your current clerkship rotation.

Open the **Browser** and navigate to the **AK_Step2_v12** tag set, then click the **!Shelf** tag and your specific rotation (ex: Peds).

Use **no_dupes** to avoid duplicates, and it will be a mixed set that includes helpful Step 1 overlap, or use the tag **only_step2** if you want strictly shelf-focused material. (From what I heard, if you are very weak on fundamentals/foundational content, you might want the step 1 cards, however, if you are coming off a step 1 dedicated like me, you are ok with step_2_only)

Once filtered, select the desired cards and **Toggle Suspend** to add them to your active study pool.

## Note
This step requires **Anking**, because the anki cards are located in that superdeck. See above steps on installation and setup of Anking!
