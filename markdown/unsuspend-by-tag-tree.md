## Finding AnKing Cards by Tag Tree

The AnKing deck is organized using a hierarchical tag system that follows the med school cirriculum & third-party resources (Boards & Beyond, Pathoma, Sketchy, etc.). This allows you to filter the full deck down to only the cards relevant to a specific lecture or topic.

To locate cards, open the **Browser** and use the **Tag Tree** on the left side. Navigate through the structure step by step from the Step 1 tag set down into your specific resource and topic.

Once you reach a section (for example a single topic like "Bile""), Anki will display all associated cards, including those currently suspended. Suspended cards appear highlighted and are not part of your active study set.

From here, you can preview cards and selectively **unsuspend** only the relevant ones to study.
