## Installing Review Heatmap

The Review Heatmap add-on adds a box-style calendar to Anki that tracks daily review activity. It is used to visually view consistency and long-term streaks. The official addon is found at https://ankiweb.net/shared/info/1771074083 

You can compare your heatmap with your friends to see who is better about keeping their daily streak!

To install it, open **Tools > Add-ons > Get Add-ons...**, paste the code **`1771074083`**, and restart Anki.

Add-on code: `1771074083`

Restart Anki after installation for the add-on to activate.
