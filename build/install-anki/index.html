Installing Anki

To install Anki, download the latest version from https://apps.ankiweb.net/ and follow the installer for your operating system. Usually, just clicking through the wizard is enough to get it working. Once I had an issue with a missing driver/library, but I was booting from Linux. If you are unable to get it working, feel free to reach out!
